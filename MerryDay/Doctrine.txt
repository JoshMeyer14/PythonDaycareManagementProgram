What We Believe

As members of the Christian and Missionary Alliance, Deltona Alliance Church adheres to the Statement of Faith that is set forth by the C&MA and adopted by all Alliance churches at General Council.

THERE IS ONE GOD,
who is infinitely perfect, existing eternally in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Reference: Deuteronomy 6:4, Matthew 5:48, Matthew 28:19

JESUS CHRIST IS THE TRUE GOD AND THE TRUE MAN.

THE HOLY SPIRIT IS A DIVINE PERSON.

THE OLD AND NEW TESTAMENTS.

MAN WAS ORIGINALLY CREATED IN THE IMAGE AND LIKENESS OF GOD.

SALVATION HAS BEEN PROVIDED THROUGH JESUS CHRIST FOR ALL MEN.

IT IS THE WILL OF GOD THAT EACH BELIEVER SHOULD BE FILLED WITH THE HOLY SPIRIT.

PROVISION IS MADE IN THE REDEMPTIVE WORK OF THE LORD JESUS CHRIST.

THE CHURCH CONSISTS OF ALL THOSE WHO BELIEVE ON THE LORD JESUS CHRIST.

THERE SHALL BE A BODILY RESURRECTION OF THE JUST AND OF THE UNJUST.

THE SECOND COMING OF THE LORD JESUS CHRIST IS IMMINENT.
